Experience world-class service at Amaya Hills Kandy

Located in Kandy, Amaya Hills, a leading 5-star hotel in Kandy, offers a hillside retreat overlooking the Heerassagala forest. Known as one of the best luxury hotels in Kandy, this hotel features traditional Sri Lankan architecture, an outdoor pool, a spa and a restaurant.

This Kandy hotel also offers air-conditioned rooms, including private balconies with mountain or valley views. Rooms are fitted with attached bathrooms with bathtubs, flat-screen TV and minibar.

Guests can work out at the fitness center or play a game of squash or billiards. Amaya Hills provides laundry service, tour desk and currency exchange.
